# Reason & Result — Project Management 项目管理定制版
> 来源：Check Your English Vocabulary for IELTS（第98–99页）
> **定制场景**：Project Management — 进度汇报、风险上报、变更与资源决策、干系人会议沟通
> **适用角色**：PM / 项目负责人（技术背景）

---

## 📌 核心词汇框架

### 📐 词类分类

| 词类 | 含义 | PM 场景应用 |
|------|------|-------------|
| **原因类** | 解释"为什么" | 延期原因、里程碑偏差解释、决策依据 |
| **目的类** | 表达"为了" | 资源调配目的、流程改进初衷、缓解措施意图 |
| **结果类** | 描述"结果" | 延期影响、依赖阻塞后果、交付效果 |
| **动机类** | 深层原因 | 范围取舍动机、优先级排序逻辑 |
| **影响类** | 描述"对…产生影响" | 风险对关键路径的影响、变更对范围的影响 |

## 📝 核心词汇详解 + PM 场景示例

---

### 🔵 原因类词汇 — 延期解释与决策依据

| 词汇 | 用法 | 示例 |
|------|------|------|
| **due to** | + 名词短语，表示"由于" | The release is delayed **due to** unresolved integration issues. |
| **owing to** | + 名词短语，表示"归因于" | The milestone slipped **owing to** a dependency on the vendor team. |
| **on account of** | + 名词短语，表示"因为" | Testing was postponed **on account of** the environment outage. |
| **the reason for** | + 名词/动名词，表示"…的原因" | What was **the reason for** the two-day delay in UAT sign-off? |
| **on the grounds that** | + 从句，表示"基于…的理由" | We descoped the reporting module **on the grounds that** it was not on the critical path. |

> 💡 **PM 场景提示**：这五个词在 status report、变更申请（Change Request）和风险登记册（Risk Register）中高频出现，用来解释偏差和决策理由。

**PM 实例：**

> The release is delayed **due to** unresolved integration issues between the payment service and the loyalty API.
> （本次发布因支付服务与会员积分 API 之间未解决的集成问题而延期。）

> Sprint 8 velocity dropped by 30% **owing to** two unplanned production incidents.
> （由于两次计划外的生产事故，Sprint 8 的速率下降了 30%。）

> We escalated to the steering committee **on account of** repeated scope creep from the business side.
> （由于业务侧反复出现范围蔓延，我们上报了指导委员会。）

> We deferred the data migration **on the grounds that** it carried unacceptable regression risk before go-live.
> （我们推迟了数据迁移，理由是它在上线前带有不可接受的回归风险。）

---

### 🟡 目的类词汇 — 决策与行动目的说明

| 词汇 | 用法 | 示例 |
|------|------|------|
| **in order to** | + 动词原形，表示"为了"（正式） | We reallocated two engineers **in order to** protect the critical path. |
| **so as not to** | + 动词原形，表示"为了不"（否定目的） | We froze the code base **so as not to** destabilize the release candidate. |
| **with the aim of** | + 动名词，表示"目的是" | We introduced a weekly risk review **with the aim of** catching blockers earlier. |

> 💡 **PM 场景提示**：说明资源调配、流程变更、缓解措施（mitigation）的合理性时使用，是向 sponsor 争取支持的利器。

**PM 实例：**

> We reallocated two backend engineers **in order to** unblock the integration workstream.
> （我们重新调配了两名后端工程师，以解除集成工作流的阻塞。）

> We scheduled the cutover rehearsal on Saturday **so as not to** disrupt the business-critical batch jobs.
> （我们把切换演练安排在周六，以免干扰业务关键的批处理任务。）

> We shortened the sprint to one week **with the aim of** getting faster feedback on the integration fix.
> （我们把 sprint 缩短为一周，目的是更快获得集成修复的反馈。）

---

### 🟢 结果类词汇 — 影响评估与交付效果

| 词汇 | 用法 | 示例 |
|------|------|------|
| **ensued** | v. （不及物动词）随之发生 | A two-week delay **ensued** after the vendor missed the interface deadline. |
| **as a result** | 连接词短语，表示"结果是" | The API contract changed late, and **as a result** our test cases had to be rewritten. |
| **as a consequence** | 连接词短语，表示"因此" | The integration slipped, and **as a consequence** UAT started one week late. |
| **affect** | v. 影响 | This delay will **affect** the go-live date and the marketing campaign launch. |

> 💡 **PM 场景提示**：在 impact assessment（影响评估）和干系人汇报中量化"拖延的代价"，是争取决策的关键段落。

**PM 实例：**

> If the vendor misses the next interface deadline, further slippage **will ensue** across all downstream milestones.
> （如果供应商错过下一个接口交付期限，所有下游里程碑都会随之进一步延期。）

> We added a second QA pair, and **as a result** the defect backlog was cleared within one sprint.
> （我们增加了一组 QA 资源，结果一个 sprint 内就清完了缺陷积压。）

> The environment instability **affected** three sprints' worth of testing capacity.
> （环境不稳定影响了相当于三个 sprint 的测试产能。）

---

### 🔴 动机/后果类词汇 — 干系人沟通与取舍说明

| 词汇 | 含义 | 示例 |
|------|------|------|
| **motive** | n. 动机，意图 | What was the **motive** behind pulling the feature into this release? |
| **effect** | n. 效果，影响 | The daily sync had an immediate positive **effect** on cross-team dependency resolution. |
| **consequence** | n. 后果 | We must weigh the **consequences** of going live with known defects in the reporting module. |

> 💡 **PM 场景提示**：范围取舍（trade-off）讨论、变更评审会、go/no-go 决策会议的必备词。

**PM 实例：**

> What was the **motive** behind accelerating the cutover to Q3?
> （把切换提前到 Q3 的动机是什么？）

> Removing the daily standup had a negative **effect** on early risk detection.
> （取消每日站会对早期风险发现产生了负面影响。）

> The **consequences** of shipping without the audit trail would be a compliance exposure.
> （不带审计日志上线的后果将是合规风险敞口。）

## 🎯 PM 场景句型框架

---

### 场景一：延期通知（Delay Notification）

**重点子场景：进度汇报与延期解释**

```
进度现状 → 延期原因 → 影响评估 → 补救措施 → 新预期
```

**模板：**

| 步骤 | 模板 | 示例 |
|------|------|------|
| 进度现状 | Current status: X is behind schedule by... | Current status: the integration workstream is behind schedule by two weeks. |
| 延期原因 | This is due to / owing to... | This is **due to** unresolved integration issues on the vendor side. |
| 影响评估 | As a consequence, X will affect Y... | **As a consequence**, this will **affect** UAT and push go-live to Oct 15. |
| 补救措施 | We are doing X in order to... | We are adding a contractor **in order to** compress the remaining test cycle. |
| 新预期 | As a result, we expect... | **As a result**, we expect to recover three days before the next milestone. |

**完整示例：**

> Current status: the release is delayed by two weeks **due to** unresolved integration issues between the order service and the vendor's inventory API. **As a consequence**, UAT will start one week later and **affect** the October go-live window. We have reallocated two engineers and engaged the vendor's escalation manager **in order to** protect the remaining milestones. **As a result**, we expect to recover three days by the next status meeting.

---

### 场景二：风险上报（Risk Escalation）

**重点子场景：风险识别与潜在后果沟通**

```
风险描述 → 触发原因 → 影响评估 → 缓解措施 → 预期效果
```

**模板：**

| 步骤 | 模板 |
|------|------|
| 风险描述 | There is a risk that X may occur... |
| 触发原因 | This risk arises owing to / on account of... |
| 影响评估 | If X occurs, as a consequence, ... |
| 缓解措施 | We are doing Y with the aim of... |
| 预期效果 | As a result, we expect to reduce the impact to... |

**完整示例：**

> There is a risk that the legacy data migration will overrun its window. This risk arises **owing to** undocumented data quality issues in the old system. If the overrun occurs, **as a consequence** the cutover will extend into Monday morning and **affect** day-one operations. We are running a full dry-run migration this weekend **with the aim of** validating the timing. **As a result**, we expect to reduce the residual risk to low before go-live.

---

### 场景三：变更与资源决策（Change & Resourcing Decision）

**重点子场景：说明调配人手与砍范围的动机和理由**

```
触发背景 → 备选方案 → 决策理由 → 执行动作 → 预期效果
```

**模板：**

| 步骤 | 模板 |
|------|------|
| 触发背景 | We are facing X owing to... |
| 备选方案 | Option A would..., while Option B would... |
| 决策理由 | We chose B on the grounds that... |
| 执行动作 | We reallocated / descoped X in order to... |
| 预期效果 | As a result, ... |

**完整示例：**

> We are facing a schedule crunch **owing to** the late delivery of the vendor interface. Option A was to extend the timeline by three weeks; Option B was to descope the reporting module. We chose Option B **on the grounds that** reporting is not on the critical path and can ship in a fast-follow. We reallocated those two engineers **in order to** protect the integration testing window. **As a result**, the original go-live date remains intact.

---

### 场景四：干系人会议因果陈述（Status Meeting）

**重点子场景：status meeting 上的简短因果表达**

| 想表达 | 会议口语 |
|--------|----------|
| 解释偏差 | The variance is **due to** the vendor interface slipping a week. |
| 量化影响 | **As a consequence**, UAT and go-live both shift by one week. |
| 说明目的 | We brought in a contractor **in order to** protect the test window. |
| 给出预期 | **As a result**, we expect to hold the original go-live date. |
| 说明动机 | The **motive** behind the descope was to keep go-live fixed. |

## 💬 PM 场景对话示例

---

### 对话一：向 Sponsor 解释延期两周（含补救方案）

> **PM:** Thanks for making time, Sarah. I need to walk you through a schedule change on the lululemon integration release.
>
> **Sponsor:** Okay, give it to me straight. How bad is it?
>
> **PM:** The release is delayed by two weeks **due to** unresolved integration issues between our order service and the vendor's inventory API.
>
> **Sponsor:** Two weeks? I presented October 1st to the board last month. What exactly went wrong?
>
> **PM:** The vendor delivered the interface spec late, and when we integrated it, the payload format didn't match what we'd built against. Rework **ensued** on both sides.
>
> **Sponsor:** So what's the impact beyond the two weeks?
>
> **PM:** **As a consequence**, UAT shifts by one week and go-live moves to October 15th. It doesn't **affect** the holiday freeze in November, so we still have buffer.
>
> **Sponsor:** And what are you doing about it?
>
> **PM:** Two things. First, we reallocated two backend engineers **in order to** protect the critical path. Second, we've asked the vendor to assign a dedicated engineer to our account.
>
> **Sponsor:** What's the **motive** behind pulling the engineers off the reporting workstream?
>
> **PM:** Simple trade-off: reporting isn't on the critical path, integration is. We made that call **on the grounds that** protecting go-live matters more than a parallel feature.
>
> **Sponsor:** I see. Are you confident in the new date?
>
> **PM:** Reasonably. We've added a mid-point checkpoint in ten days **with the aim of** catching any further drift early. **As a result** of the reallocation, we expect to recover three of the fourteen days.
>
> **Sponsor:** Okay. Send me the revised plan today, and flag it as amber, not red.
>
> **PM:** Will do. I'll update the risk register too, **so as not to** let the vendor dependency slip through untracked again.

---

### 对话二：风险评审会（上报集成风险，讨论缓解措施）

> **PM:** Next up on the agenda is the integration risk I logged last week. Let me walk through it.
>
> **Tech Lead:** The R-12 one? The legacy data migration, right?
>
> **PM:** Yes. There is a real risk that the migration overruns its weekend window. The trigger is **owing to** undocumented data quality issues in the legacy system.
>
> **Sponsor:** How likely is that, honestly?
>
> **PM:** Moderate probability, high impact. If the overrun occurs, **as a consequence** the cutover extends into Monday morning, which will **affect** day-one store operations. About 8% of the legacy customer records have duplicate keys, which makes the timing unpredictable.
>
> **PM:** So here's the mitigation I'm proposing. We run a full dry-run migration this weekend **with the aim of** validating the actual timing against our estimate.
>
> **Tech Lead:** I'd also add a dedup pass before the real cutover **in order to** shrink the dataset.
>
> **Sponsor:** What if the dry-run still shows we can't finish in the window?
>
> **PM:** Then we go with a phased cutover — migrate stores in two batches. We'd choose that **on the grounds that** a shorter window per batch is far lower risk than one long one.
>
> **Sponsor:** Does the phased option **affect** the business?
>
> **PM:** Minimal. Half the stores run on the old system for one extra day. We'd notify store ops in advance **so as not to** surprise anyone on the floor.
>
> **Tech Lead:** One more thing — if something goes wrong mid-cutover, what's the rollback story?
>
> **PM:** Rollback is documented and rehearsed. If we pull the trigger, downtime **ensued** last time was under forty minutes in the rehearsal.
>
> **Sponsor:** Alright. Log the phased cutover as the contingency plan, and get the dry-run booked for Saturday.
>
> **PM:** Done. **As a result** of these mitigations, I'll downgrade the residual risk from high to medium after the dry-run results come in.

---

### 对话三：资源协调会议（向其他团队负责人借调人手）

> **PM:** Mark, do you have fifteen minutes? I'd like to borrow some of your team's time.
>
> **Platform Lead:** Depends what you mean by "borrow." What do you need?
>
> **PM:** Two backend engineers for two weeks, starting Monday. The integration workstream is blocked **owing to** the vendor's late interface delivery.
>
> **Platform Lead:** You know my team is already committed to the identity migration until the end of the sprint.
>
> **PM:** I know, and I wouldn't ask if it weren't on the critical path. Integration slips will **affect** the October go-live directly. They'd rewrite the payload mapping layer and run the contract tests — contained work with a clear definition of done.
>
> **Platform Lead:** What's the reason for the urgency? Can't your own team absorb it?
>
> **PM:** My team is at capacity with UAT prep. The **motive** for coming to you is that your people built the original API gateway — they know this integration inside out.
>
> **Platform Lead:** That's fair. But what do I get back?
>
> **PM:** I'll return two QA days from my team to your migration testing **in order to** keep your timeline intact.
>
> **Platform Lead:** Hmm. And who backfills their identity migration tasks?
>
> **PM:** We descope the reporting module from this release, so those two engineers are genuinely free. I made the descope call **on the grounds that** reporting isn't on the critical path.
>
> **Platform Lead:** In that case, I can give you Priya and Chen from Monday **so as not to** break our own sprint commitment — they're between tasks until Wednesday anyway.
>
> **PM:** Two weeks, Monday start. Perfect.
>
> **Platform Lead:** One condition: daily async updates in our shared channel **with the aim of** avoiding surprise status changes.
>
> **PM:** Agreed. I'll add them to the standup as optional attendees. **As a result**, both workstreams stay visible.
>
> **Platform Lead:** Deal. Send me the request through the resource board so it's on record.
>
> **PM:** Will do — submitting it now **so as not to** rely on a verbal agreement.---

## 📊 PM 场景速查表

### 进度汇报（周报 / Status Update）

| 想表达 | 常用表达 |
|--------|----------|
| **进度延期** | The release is delayed / behind schedule due to... |
| **偏差原因** | The variance is owing to / on account of... |
| **影响评估** | As a consequence, this will affect X / push go-live to... |
| **补救措施** | We are doing X in order to protect the critical path. |
| **新预期** | As a result, we expect to recover X days by... |
| **里程碑状态** | Milestone M2 slipped owing to a vendor dependency. |

### 风险上报（Risk Register / 评审会）

| 想表达 | 常用表达 |
|--------|----------|
| **描述风险** | There is a risk that X may occur... |
| **触发原因** | This risk arises owing to / on account of... |
| **潜在后果** | If X occurs, as a consequence, ... |
| **缓解措施** | We are doing Y with the aim of reducing the impact. |
| **残余风险** | As a result, residual risk drops to medium/low. |
| **决策理由** | We chose the phased approach on the grounds that... |

### 资源协调（借调 / Descope / 变更）

| 想表达 | 常用表达 |
|--------|----------|
| **提出请求** | I'd like to borrow X's time for two weeks. |

## ⚠️ 常见错误对照

| ❌ 避免 | ✅ 推荐 |
|--------|--------|
| "Due to we lost two engineers..." | "Due to losing two engineers..." / "Owing to the loss of two engineers..." |
| "The risk will affect to the go-live date." | "The risk will affect the go-live date."（affect 是及物动词，不加 to） |
| "In order to protect the deadline, so we reallocated..." | "In order to protect the deadline, we reallocated..."（不要同时用 so） |
| "On the ground that it's risky..." | "On the grounds that it's risky..."（固定短语用复数 grounds） |
