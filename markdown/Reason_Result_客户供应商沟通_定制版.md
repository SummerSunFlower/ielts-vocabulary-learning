# Reason & Result — 客户与供应商沟通定制版（对外正式场景）
> 来源：Check Your English Vocabulary for IELTS（第98–99页）
> **定制场景**：Softtek L2-L3 技术支持工程师 → 服务 lululemon（零售客户）的对外沟通
> **语言特点**：正式、委婉、外交式 —— 对内可以直说 "it broke"，对外必须 "We regret to inform you..."

---

## 📌 核心词汇框架

### 📐 词类分类

| 词类 | 含义 | 对外沟通应用 |
|------|------|----------------|
| **原因类** | 解释"为什么" | 延误原因、事故根因、变更理由的正式陈述 |
| **目的类** | 表达"为了" | 补救措施目的、预防承诺、变更收益说明 |
| **结果类** | 描述"结果" | 影响评估、服务状态恢复、SLA 影响说明 |
| **动机类** | 深层原因 | 向客户解释决策动机、向供应商追责 |
| **后果类** | 名词性影响 | 谈判中的后果声明、期望管理 |

> 💡 **对外沟通提示**：对外文件中原因词要更正式（owing to > because of），结果词要客观（as a consequence 比 so 更外交），追责时要"对事不对人"。

---

## 📝 核心词汇详解 + 对外沟通示例

---

### 🔵 原因类词汇 — 正式解释原因

| 词汇 | 用法 | 正式度 | 示例 |
|------|------|--------|------|
| **due to** | + 名词短语 | ★★★ | The delay was **due to** an unforeseen infrastructure issue. |
| **owing to** | + 名词短语 | ★★★★★ | **Owing to** circumstances beyond our control, the rollout has been postponed. |
| **on account of** | + 名词短语 | ★★★★ | Service was suspended **on account of** scheduled maintenance. |
| **the reason for** | + 名词/动名词 | ★★★ | We would like to clarify **the reason for** this adjustment. |
| **on the grounds that** | + 从句 | ★★★★★ | The vendor declined the request **on the grounds that** it fell outside the agreed scope. |

> 💡 **对外提示**：`owing to` 和 `on the grounds that` 是对外邮件和合同沟通的首选；`because of` 偏口语，慎用。

**客户与供应商沟通实例：**

> We regret to inform you that the delivery of the new POS integration has been delayed **owing to** a third-party dependency that was outside our control.
> （我们遗憾地通知您，由于一项超出我们控制范围的第三方依赖，新 POS 集成的交付已延期。）

> The maintenance window was rescheduled **on account of** the client's peak trading hours.
> （由于客户的营业高峰时段，维护窗口已重新安排。）

> We escalated the issue to the vendor **on the grounds that** the recurring failures breached Clause 4.2 of the SLA.
> （我们已将该问题升级给供应商，理由是反复出现的故障违反了 SLA 第 4.2 条。）

---

### 🟡 目的类词汇 — 补救措施与预防承诺

| 词汇 | 用法 | 示例 |
|------|------|------|
| **in order to** | + 动词原形，"为了"（正式） | We have deployed a workaround **in order to** minimize disruption to your stores. |
| **so as not to** | + 动词原形，"为了不" | We will phase the rollout **so as not to** affect peak trading hours. |
| **with the aim of** | + 动名词，"目的是" | We are conducting a full review **with the aim of** preventing recurrence. |

> 💡 **对外提示**：这三个词是"补救承诺三件套"——致歉之后必须紧跟着 "In order to / With the aim of..."，向客户展示行动，而不只是道歉。

**客户与供应商沟通实例：**

> To minimize the impact, we have implemented a temporary workaround **in order to** keep all stores operational.
> （为将影响降到最低，我们已实施临时变通方案，以确保所有门店正常运营。）

> The upgrade will be performed overnight **so as not to** disrupt your business hours.
> （升级将在夜间进行，以免影响您的营业时间。）

> We have engaged our senior engineering team **with the aim of** completing the remediation plan by Friday.
> （我们已调派高级工程团队介入，目标是在周五前完成整改计划。）

---

### 🟢 结果类词汇 — 影响评估与状态更新

| 词汇 | 用法 | 示例 |
|------|------|------|
| **ensued** | v. 随之发生 | A brief system outage **ensued**, during which online orders could not be processed. |
| **as a result** | 连接短语，"结果是" | **As a result**, you can expect the revised delivery by October 15. |
| **as a consequence** | 连接短语，"因此"（更正式） | **As a consequence**, SLA credits will be applied to your next invoice. |
| **affect** | v. 影响 | This change will **affect** approximately 3% of store transactions. |

**客户与供应商沟通实例：**

> The database failure **affected** order processing for 47 minutes between 14:02 and 14:49 local time.
> （数据库故障在当地时间 14:02 至 14:49 期间影响了订单处理，共计 47 分钟。）

> The incident has been fully resolved, and **as a result**, all services have returned to normal operation.
> （事故已完全解决，因此所有服务已恢复正常运行。）

> **As a consequence of** the extended downtime, we have proactively applied service credits in accordance with Section 6 of the agreement.
> （鉴于停机时间延长，我们已根据协议第 6 条主动为贵方申请了服务补偿金。）

> A temporary degradation in checkout performance **ensued**, and we monitored the situation closely until stability was confirmed.
> （随后出现了结账性能的暂时下降，我们持续监控直至确认稳定。）

---

### 🔴 动机/后果类词汇 — 谈判与追责

| 词汇 | 含义 | 示例 |
|------|------|------|
| **motive** | n. 动机 | We would like to understand the vendor's **motive** for proposing this change at this stage. |
| **effect** | n. 效果，影响 | The new caching layer had an immediate positive **effect** on page load times. |
| **consequence** | n. 后果 | We must consider the **consequences** of missing the go-live date for the holiday season. |

**客户与供应商沟通实例：**

> Could you help us understand the **motive** behind recommending the architecture change now rather than after the peak season?
> （能否请您说明，为什么建议在旺季前而非旺季后再进行架构调整？）

> The vendor's repeated missed deadlines will have a serious **effect** on our client's holiday campaign readiness.
> （供应商屡次错过截止日期，将对我们客户的假日活动筹备产生严重影响。）

> Should the issue recur, we would have no alternative **consequence** to consider other than invoking the termination clause.
> （如果问题再次发生，我们将不得不考虑启动合同终止条款的后果。）

## 🎭 对外沟通四大语气工具箱

| 工具 | 功能 | 代表句式 |
|------|------|----------|
| **缓冲语** | 坏消息前先铺垫 | We regret to inform you that, **owing to** unforeseen complications, the release has been postponed. |
| **致歉** | 承认影响，维护关系 | We sincerely apologize for any inconvenience caused by this delay. |
| **补救承诺** | 致歉后必跟行动 | **In order to** prevent recurrence, we will implement additional pre-release testing. |
| **期望管理** | 给出可预期结果 | We anticipate that this will **affect** batch reporting only; **as a result**, the revised date is October 15. |

> 💡 **核心公式**：缓冲语 → 客观原因（due to/owing to）→ 影响评估（affect）→ 补救承诺（in order to/with the aim of）→ 预期结果（as a result）。坏消息永远不是孤立出现的。

## 🎯 四大对外子场景句型框架

### 场景一：延误通知（Delay Notification） & 场景二：SLA 违约沟通（SLA Breach）

| 场景 | 结构链 | 关键句示例 |
|------|--------|------------|
| **延误通知** | 致歉缓冲 → 延期原因 → 影响评估 → 补救措施 → 新时间表 | We regret to inform you that the go-live date must be moved, **owing to** unresolved data migration issues identified during UAT. We anticipate this will **affect** the Phase 2 rollout only. **In order to** recover the schedule, we have added a second migration team. **As a result**, the revised go-live date is November 3. |
| **SLA 违约沟通** | 事实陈述（数据）→ 影响 → SLA 条款依据 → 要求补偿/行动 → 升级路径 | Our records show 3 P1 incidents this month, each breaching the 1-hour target. This has **affected** store checkout operations across 12 locations. **In accordance with** Section 6.1, these breaches qualify for service credits. We would therefore ask that the credits be reflected on the November invoice. Should this pattern continue, we will escalate to the account management level. |

---

### 场景三：变更请求说明 & 场景四：期望管理与跟进

| 场景 | 结构链 | 关键句示例 |
|------|--------|------------|
| **变更请求** | 变更动机 → 原因 → 影响评估 → 客户利益 → 风险缓解 | The **motive** behind this change is to improve peak-season stability, **due to** recurring timeouts last season. This will **affect** read-only access for 2 hours. **As a result**, response times will improve by 30%. The rollout is overnight **so as not to** disrupt trading hours. |
| **期望管理** | 状态确认 → 进展原因 → 已完成动作 → 下一步 → 预期结果 | We would like to provide an update on... The remaining effort is **due to** final regression testing. **With the aim of** keeping you informed, we will send daily updates. **As a result**, we remain confident in the agreed date. |

### 📧 文书一：延误通知邮件（完整商务邮件格式）

> **Subject:** Project Update — Revised Delivery Date for Inventory Sync Module
>
> Dear Ms. Chen,
>
> I am writing to inform you of an adjustment to the delivery schedule for the Inventory Sync Module, currently in development for your retail operations team.
>
> We regret to inform you that, **owing to** complications identified during user acceptance testing, we are unable to meet the originally agreed delivery date of September 20.
>
> The primary cause of this delay is a data mapping conflict **due to** legacy SKU formats in your ERP system. This issue **affected** the accuracy of real-time stock updates in our test environment, and we consider it essential to resolve before go-live.
>
> We anticipate that this will **affect** the delivery date by approximately two weeks. Phase 1 store deployments remain unaffected, **as a consequence of** the phased delivery structure agreed in the project plan.
>
> **In order to** recover as much of the schedule as possible, we have assigned two additional engineers to the data mapping workstream and extended our testing coverage **with the aim of** ensuring a stable release.
>
> **As a result**, you can expect the revised delivery date to be October 4, with go-live validation completed by October 8. We will confirm these dates again in our weekly status call.
>
> We sincerely apologize for any inconvenience caused by this delay, and we appreciate your understanding **on account of** the complexity of the legacy integration involved.
>
> Should you have any questions or wish to discuss the revised schedule, please do not hesitate to contact me directly at any time.
>
> Kind regards,
> Zhou Jie
> L2 Technical Support Engineer, Softtek
> Service Delivery Team — lululemon Account

---

### 📞 对话二：与供应商的 SLA 违约电话会议

> **Softtek (You):** Good morning, David. I've called this review meeting **on the grounds that** we've recorded three separate SLA breaches this month, and we need to align on both the root causes and the remedial actions.
>
> **Vendor (David):** Good morning. We're aware of the incidents, though I'd push back slightly — two of the three were resolved within the window.
>
> **Softtek:** With respect, the *response* time was within the window, but the *resolution* time was not. Our data shows the checkout API was degraded for a combined 6.5 hours. That's what **affected** our client's stores.
>
> **Vendor:** The third incident was **due to** an unusual traffic pattern on your side. We hadn't planned capacity for that.
>
> **Softtek:** I understand your position, but the traffic pattern followed your own recommended promotion module rollout. Could you clarify your **motive** for advising that rollout without a corresponding capacity plan?
>
> **Vendor:** That's... a fair question. The recommendation came from our solutions team, and I accept there was a gap in the handover.
>
> **Softtek:** Thank you for acknowledging that. So, as things stand: three breaches, 6.5 hours of degraded service, and store operations **affected** across twelve locations. **As a consequence**, Section 6.1 of our agreement entitles us to service credits for this period.
>
> **Vendor:** We'd prefer to discuss a goodwill gesture instead of formal credits, given the strong relationship.
>
> **Softtek:** We value the relationship as well, which is precisely why we're raising this formally. However, the credits are contractual — **on the grounds that** the breaches are documented in our incident reports, we would ask that they be applied to the next invoice.
>
> **Vendor:** Let me take the credits calculation back to our account team. What about prevention?
>
> **Softtek:** That's the more important part. **In order to** prevent recurrence, we'd like a written remediation plan covering capacity planning, change handover, and a revised escalation matrix **with the aim of** reaching your P1 team directly.
>
> **Vendor:** We can have the remediation plan to you within five business days, and we'll add your P1 queue as a direct paging target.
>
> **Softtek:** Good. **As a result** of today's commitments, we'd expect measurable improvement within the next two billing cycles. Should the pattern continue, we'll escalate to the executive level on both sides. To be clear, our aim is a stable service, not penalties — but we do need the data and the commitments in writing. I'll send a summary email after this call.

### 📧 文书三：服务事故后的客户安抚邮件

> **Subject:** Incident Notification & Resolution Summary — Checkout Service Degradation (Oct 12)
>
> Dear Mr. Alvarez,
>
> I am writing to provide a full account of the service degradation **affecting** your checkout systems on October 12, together with the steps we have taken since. Unfortunately, **due to** a certificate expiry on one of our payment gateway nodes, checkout transactions were intermittently unavailable between 09:14 and 10:02 local time.
>
> During this window, a partial outage **ensued** in which approximately 8% of transactions were delayed or required retry. Store staff were able to use the offline mode as a workaround, and we have confirmed that no transaction data was lost. We sincerely apologize for the disruption this has caused to your store operations during morning trading hours.
>
> To minimize the immediate impact, our team renewed the certificate and deployed an automated expiry alert **in order to** ensure this specific failure mode cannot occur unnoticed again. **With the aim of** preventing recurrence more broadly, we are completing the following actions by October 20:
>
> 1. Automated certificate monitoring across all payment nodes, with 30-day advance alerts;
> 2. A failover validation exercise **so as not to** leave any single point of failure in the payment path;
> 3. An updated incident runbook shared with your IT team for transparency.
>
> **As a consequence of** the 48-minute service interruption, and in accordance with Section 6 of our service agreement, a service credit has been applied to your October invoice. We have taken this step proactively; no claim is required on your side. **As a result**, you can expect full monitoring coverage of all payment path components going forward, and we will include certificate status in your monthly service report.
>
> We apologize once again for the inconvenience caused. Should you wish to review the full incident timeline or discuss any of the preventive measures, I would be glad to arrange a call at your convenience.
>
> Kind regards,
> Zhou Jie
> L2 Technical Support Engineer, Softtek
> Service Delivery Team — lululemon Account

## 📋 两大句型模板（可直接套用）

---

### 模板一：延误通知邮件模板

```
[致歉缓冲] We regret to inform you that...
[延期原因] ...owing to / due to [具体、客观的原因——避免归咎于人]
[影响评估] We anticipate that this will affect [范围], while [不受影响的部分] remains unaffected.
[补救措施] In order to [恢复目标], we have [行动 1] and [行动 2].
[额外承诺] With the aim of preventing recurrence, we will [流程改进].
[新时间表] As a result, you can expect [新日期/里程碑].
[再次致歉] We sincerely apologize for any inconvenience caused by this delay.
[联系渠道] Should you have any questions, please do not hesitate to contact me.
```

### 模板二：SLA 沟通模板（对供应商追责）

```
[事实陈述] Our records show that [X 次违约 / Y 小时降级], based on [监控数据/工单记录].
[影响说明] This has affected [业务范围], resulting in [具体业务后果].
[条款依据] In accordance with Section [N] of the agreement, ... / on the grounds that ...
[正式要求] We would therefore ask that [credits / remediation plan / escalation path].
[预防要求] In order to prevent recurrence, we require [具体预防动作].
[升级声明] Should this pattern continue, we will escalate to [更高层级].
[外交收尾] Our aim is a stable service, not penalties — but we need commitments in writing.
```

## ⚖️ 语气分级表：对内 Slack vs 对外邮件

| 想表达 | ❌ 对内 Slack（直白） | ✅ 对外邮件（外交式） |
|--------|----------------------|----------------------|
| **系统挂了** | prod is down, checking | We regret to inform you that a service degradation occurred. |
| **是我们的锅** | yeah we broke it, my bad | Unfortunately, due to an error in our release process... |
| **要延期** | won't make Friday, sorry | We regret to inform you that the delivery date has been revised. |
| **供应商的锅** | vendor dropped the ball again | The issue originated from a third-party dependency. |
| **再出事就走人** | next time we're done | Should this pattern continue, we will consider further contractual measures. |
| **要补偿** | we want credits for this | In accordance with Section 6, we would ask that service credits be applied. |

> 💡 **核心原则**：对内求速度，对外求关系。对外邮件中，事实要精确（时间、百分比、条款号），情绪要抽离（用 as a consequence 代替 we're angry）。

## ⚠️ 常见错误对照：过于生硬 vs 委婉专业

| ❌ 过于生硬/直接 | ✅ 委婉专业 |
|------------------|------------|
| "Your system caused the delay." | "The delay was **due to** a compatibility issue with the legacy system." |
| "You missed the SLA again. Pay up." | "**As a consequence of** the documented breaches, service credits will be applied in accordance with Section 6." |
| "This is your fault, not ours." | "**Owing to** circumstances beyond our control, including a third-party dependency..." |
| "We can't do anything until you sign off." | "We are unable to proceed **on the grounds that** formal approval is required under the change process." |
| "Why did you do that?" | "Could you help us understand the **motive** behind this recommendation?" |
| "It'll probably break again." | "We anticipate that residual risk remains, and **with the aim of** mitigating it, additional monitoring has been deployed." |

> 💡 **技巧**：生硬句子往往缺少"原因词"和"缓冲语"。加一个 `due to / owing to / on the grounds that`，再用 `We regret / unfortunately / we would appreciate` 软化开头，语气立刻专业。

## 💡 使用建议

### 场景适配

| 沟通对象 | 推荐词汇 | 语气要点 |
|----------|----------|----------|
| **客户（lululemon）邮件** | owing to, as a consequence, with the aim of | 致歉在前，补救在后，条款号要引用 |
| **供应商电话/会议** | on the grounds that, as a consequence, motive | 事实+数据先行，追责对事不对人 |
| **变更通知** | in order to, so as not to, affect, as a result | 强调客户利益与风险缓解 |
| **进展周报/跟进** | due to, as a result, with the aim of | 展示进展，管理预期，给出确定日期 |

### 外交式沟通三原则

| 原则 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| **先缓冲，后坏消息** | We regret to inform you that... 永远放在坏消息之前 |
| **原因客观化** | 用 due to a process gap 代替 because of our mistake；用第三方依赖做缓冲但不撒谎 |
| **道歉必带行动** | 每句 apologize 后面必须跟 in order to / with the aim of 的补救承诺 |
