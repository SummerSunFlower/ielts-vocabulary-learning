# 📐 Changes — PM 项目管理定制版

> 基于《Check Your English Vocabulary for IELTS》第12-17页 · 适用角色：PM / 项目负责人（技术背景）

## 核心词汇框架

本章词汇分两大类：一类描述**进度与指标的趋势/数据变化**（Changes 1），一类描述**范围、资源、风险等状态/性质的变化**（Changes 2）。在 PM 汇报里，这两类往往成对出现——先说指标"怎么变"，再说状态"为什么变"。

### 词类分类

| 词类 | 含义 | PM 场景应用 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **上升类动词** | 指标/数值变大 | 进度偏差扩大、成本上升、burn rate 走高、风险敞口增加 |
| **下降类动词** | 指标/数值变小 | 进度追赶、缺陷数下降、资源释放、预算消耗减少 |
| **波动/持平** | 不稳定或不变 | 燃尽图波动、范围基本稳定、关键指标持平 |
| **程度副词** | 描述变化幅度 | dramatically / sharply / slightly 等修饰偏差与风险变化 |
| **趋势名词** | 概括走向 | upward / downward trend 用于周报总结 |
| **状态变化动词** | 性质/状态改变 | 范围调整、团队扩缩、风险恶化/缓解、流程转型 |

## 进度与指标趋势词汇（Changes 1）

用于描述进度偏差、燃尽图、预算消耗、范围变化等"数据怎么变"。每项配 PM 场景例句，英文句后紧跟中文翻译。

| 词汇 | 用法 | 示例（英文 + 中文） |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `increase` | v./n. 上升 | The schedule slipped and the variance **increased** to 12 days.（进度延期，偏差扩大到12天。） |
| `rise` | v./n. 上升（不及物） | The open defect count **rose** from 9 to 23 after regressions.（回归后未关闭缺陷数从9升到23。） |
| `go up` | 短语 上升 | The burn rate **went up** once the contractor joined the team.（外包人员加入后，消耗速率上升了。） |
| `peak at` | 短语 在…达到峰值 | The backlog **peaked at** 84 items during the hardening sprint.（在加固冲刺期间，待办积压在84项达到峰值。） |
| `reach a peak of` | 短语 达到…的峰值 | The defect inflow **reached a peak of** 31 in week three.（缺陷流入在第三周达到31个的峰值。） |
| `decrease` | v./n. 下降 | Once the hotfix shipped, the open risk count **decreased** by 40%.（热修复上线后，未关闭风险数下降了40%。） |
| `decline` | v./n. 下降（渐弱） | The test pass rate began to **decline** after the environment changed.（环境变更后，测试通过率开始下降。） |
| `drop` | v./n. 下降（骤降） | The remaining buffer **dropped** from 9 to 3 days in one week.（剩余缓冲在一周内从9天骤降到3天。） |
| `fall` | v./n. 下降（不及物） | Team velocity **fell** below the release forecast for two sprints.（团队速率连续两个冲刺低于发布预测。） |
| `go down` | 短语 下降 | The escape defect rate **went down** after we added contract tests.（加入契约测试后，逃逸缺陷率下降了。） |
| `fluctuate` | v. 波动 | The daily throughput **fluctuated** between 12 and 19 story points.（每日吞吐量在12到19个故事点之间波动。） |
| `remain constant` | 短语 保持恒定 | The baseline budget **remained constant** across all four quarters.（基线预算在四个季度内保持恒定。） |
| `remain steady` | 短语 保持稳定 | The defect closure rate **remained steady** at 15 per week.（缺陷关闭率稳定在每周15个。） |
| `dramatically` | adv. 剧烈地 | The schedule variance grew **dramatically** after the vendor delay.（供应商延期后，进度偏差剧烈扩大。） |
| `sharply` | adv. 急剧地 | The risk exposure **sharply** increased when the key engineer left.（核心工程师离开时，风险敞口急剧上升。） |
| `steadily` | adv. 稳定地 | The release confidence **steadily** improved through the hardening phase.（在加固阶段，发布信心稳步提升。） |
| `gradually` | adv. 逐渐地 | The scope was **gradually** trimmed to protect the go-live date.（范围被逐步削减以保护上线日期。） |
| `slightly` | adv. 轻微地 | The budget slipped **slightly**, by less than 2% of the total.（预算轻微超支，不到总额的2%。） |
| `upward trend` | 名词短语 上升趋势 | There is an **upward trend** in the number of integration defects.（集成缺陷数量呈上升趋势。） |
| `downward trend` | 名词短语 下降趋势 | The burn-down chart shows a **downward trend** in remaining work.（燃尽图显示剩余工作量呈下降趋势。） |

> **💡 PM 场景提示：** 这些词在 status report、dashboard、risk register 中高频出现。用程度副词（dramatically / sharply / slightly）量化"变化有多大"，比含糊说 "a lot" 更专业。
>
> The schedule variance **increased** from 4 to 12 days and the remaining buffer **dropped** to 3 days.（进度偏差从4天扩大到12天，剩余缓冲骤降到3天。）
>
> The defect inflow **rose** sharply, then **peaked at** 31 before the hotfix shipped.（缺陷流入急剧上升，在热修复上线前达到31个的峰值。）

## 范围/资源/风险状态变化动词（Changes 2）

用于描述范围、团队、风险、流程等"状态怎么变"。Group 1 偏中性的"改变"，Group 2 偏带有方向/结果的"转变"。

### Group 1 — 中性变化动词

| 词汇 | 用法 | 示例（英文 + 中文） |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `adjust` | v. 调整 / 适应 | We **adjusted** the milestone dates to reflect the new dependency order.（我们调整了里程碑日期以反映新的依赖顺序。） |
| `alter` | v. 改变 | The API contract was **altered** after the security review.（安全评审后，API 契约被改变了。） |
| `deteriorate` | v. 恶化 | The delivery risk **deteriorated** when the vendor missed two handoffs.（供应商两次未交付后，交付风险恶化了。） |
| `exchange` | v. 交换 | We **exchanged** the UI engineer for a backend engineer on the critical path.（我们用一名后端工程师换下了关键路径上的一名前端工程师。） |
| `fade` | v. 逐渐消失 / 褪色 | Stakeholder interest began to **fade** as the timeline slipped.（随着时间线延期，干系人的兴趣开始减退。） |
| `reduce` | v. 减少 | We **reduced** the test scope to shorten the release cycle.（我们缩减了测试范围以缩短发布周期。） |
| `renovate` | v. 翻新 | We will **renovate** the legacy build pipeline to cut deploy time.（我们将翻新遗留构建流水线以缩短部署时间。） |
| `swell` | v. 膨胀 / 增大 | The backlog began to **swell** after the support team raised new tickets.（支持团队提交新工单后，待办积压开始膨胀。） |
| `switch` | v. 转换 | We **switched** to a weekly cadence to catch drift earlier.（我们切换到每周节奏以便更早发现问题。） |
| `switch to` | 短语 切换到 | We **switched to** a cloud staging environment to speed up UAT.（我们切换到云端预发环境以加快 UAT。） |
| `vary` | v. 变化 / 不同 | Team capacity will **vary** by 20% across the holiday period.（假期期间团队产能会有最高20%的波动。） |

### Group 2 — 带方向/结果的变化动词

| 词汇 | 用法 | 示例（英文 + 中文） |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `adapt` | v. 适应 / 改编 | The team **adapted** the plan when the integration broke late.（集成在后期出问题时，团队调整适应了计划。） |
| `cut` | v. 削减 | We **cut** two low-priority features to protect the critical path.（我们砍掉两个低优先级功能以保护关键路径。） |
| `decline` | v. 下降 / 拒绝 | The sponsor **declined** the change request to avoid scope creep.（赞助人拒绝了变更请求以避免范围蔓延。） |
| `disappear` | v. 消失 | The blocker **disappeared** once the environment was restored.（环境恢复后，阻塞点消失了。） |
| `expand` | v. 扩张 | We will **expand** the QA pod by two engineers for the release.（我们将为发布给 QA 小组扩充两名工程师。） |
| `improve` | v. 改善 | The deployment process **improved** after we added canaries.（加入金丝雀发布后，部署流程改善了。） |
| `promote` | v. 提升 / 晋升 | We **promoted** the candidate build to production after sign-off.（签核后，我们将候选版本提升发布到生产环境。） |
| `relax` | v. 放松 | We can **relax** the review gate once the flaky tests are fixed.（易碎测试修复后，我们可以放宽评审门槛。） |
| `replace` | v. 替换 | We **replaced** the manual step with an automated pipeline.（我们用自动化流水线替换了手工步骤。） |
| `transform` | v. 彻底转型 / 改变 | The incident process was **transformed** into a blameless workflow.（事件处理流程被彻底转型为无责复盘工作流。） |

> **💡 PM 场景提示：** 范围/资源/风险的状态变化是变更管理（Change Management）的核心。描述"为什么变"用 adjust / alter / switch，"变好/变差"用 improve / deteriorate / decline，"变多/变少"用 expand / cut / reduce。
>
> We **cut** two low-priority features and **expanded** the QA pod to protect the release.（我们砍掉两个低优先级功能并扩充了 QA 小组以保护发布。）

## 场景一：进度趋势汇报（Progress Trend Report）

**流程：** 现状 → 偏差趋势 → 根因 → 影响 → 补救

| 步骤 | 模板 | 示例 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **进度现状** | The schedule is behind by X days. | The schedule is behind by six working days against the baseline. |
| **偏差趋势** | The variance increased / rose / went up to... | The variance **increased** from 4 to 12 days and then **peaked at** 12. |
| **根因** | This is because / driven by... | This is driven by the late interface delivery and rework on both sides. |
| **影响** | As a result, X will be affected / pushed... | As a result, UAT shifts by a week and the buffer **dropped** to 3 days. |
| **补救** | We adjusted / switched to / added... in order to... | We **switched to** a weekly sync and **adjusted** the milestone in order to recover. |

> **完整示例：** The schedule is behind by six working days against the baseline. The variance **increased** from 4 to 12 days after the vendor missed two handoffs, then **peaked at** 12 before the hotfix landed. The root cause was late interface delivery and contract mismatches, so rework **deteriorated** the delivery risk. As a result, UAT shifted by one week and the remaining buffer **dropped** to three days. To recover, we **adjusted** the milestone dates, **switched to** a weekly cadence, and **added** a contractor to compress the test cycle. The burn-down now shows a **downward trend**, so we expect to recover four of the twelve days by the next status meeting.

## 场景二：范围/资源调整通报（Scope & Resource Adjustment）

**流程：** 触发原因 → 范围动作 → 资源动作 → 流程动作 → 预期结果

| 步骤 | 模板 |
| --- | --- |
| **触发原因** | To protect the critical path / go-live date, we... |
| **范围动作** | We expanded / reduced / cut / replaced the scope... |
| **资源动作** | We expanded / exchanged / reallocated the team... |
| **流程动作** | We transformed / renovated / switched to the process... |
| **预期结果** | As a result, the risk rating / delivery confidence... |

> **完整示例：** To protect the go-live date, we **expanded** the QA pod by two engineers and **reduced** the test scope on non-critical paths. We **replaced** the manual release checklist with an automated pipeline, and we **exchanged** a UI engineer for a backend engineer on the critical path. We also **transformed** our nightly build into a continuous delivery flow and **renovated** the legacy staging environment to cut deploy time. As a result, the schedule variance **fell** below one week and delivery confidence **steadily improved**. The remaining work now shows a clear **downward trend** on the burn-down chart.

## 对话一：向 Sponsor 解释进度偏差扩大并说明资源调整

> **PM:** Thanks for the time. I need to walk you through a schedule change on the integration release.
>
> **Sponsor:** Give it to me straight. How bad is it?
>
> **PM:** The variance **increased** from 4 to 12 days after the vendor missed two handoffs.
>
> **Sponsor:** Twelve days? I briefed the board on a fixed date last month. What happened?
>
> **PM:** The late interface delivery forced rework, and our buffer **dropped** from 9 to 3 days in a single week. Worse, the delivery risk **deteriorated** when the contract tests didn't match the real payload.
>
> **Sponsor:** And what are you doing about the team?
>
> **PM:** We **adjusted** the staffing: we pulled two engineers off the reporting workstream and **switched to** a weekly sync to catch drift earlier. Reporting isn't on the critical path, integration is.
>
> **Sponsor:** Confident in the new date?
>
> **PM:** Reasonably. The burn-down now shows a **downward trend**, so I expect to recover four of the twelve days by the next status meeting.

## 对话二：风险评审会（风险等级演变）

> **PM:** Next is the integration risk I logged last week. The trigger was undocumented data quality issues in the legacy system, and the rating had been high.
>
> **Sponsor:** Did the dry-run help?
>
> **PM:** Yes. After we fixed the dedup pass, the residual risk **improved** from high to medium, and the probability **dropped** sharply.
>
> **Tech Lead:** We also **relaxed** the review gate on the non-critical paths now that the flaky tests are stable.
>
> **PM:** Right. The team **adapted** the plan when the integration broke late, and we **switched** to a phased cutover to lower the window per batch. As a result, I'll downgrade the risk rating to low after this meeting.
>
> **Sponsor:** Good. Keep the rating amber until the cutover actually completes.

## PM 场景速查表

### 进度汇报（周报 / Status Update）

| 想表达 | 常用表达 |
| --- | --- |
| **偏差扩大** | The variance increased / rose / went up to 12 days. |
| **缓冲骤降** | The remaining buffer dropped from 9 to 3 days. |
| **达到峰值** | The backlog peaked at 84 items during the sprint. |
| **趋势总结** | The burn-down shows a downward trend in remaining work. |
| **幅度修饰** | The risk exposure sharply increased when the lead left. |

| 想表达 | 常用表达 |
| --- | --- |
| **风险恶化** | The delivery risk deteriorated after the missed handoffs. |
| **风险缓解** | The residual risk improved from high to medium. |
| **放宽控制** | We can relax the review gate once flaky tests are fixed. |
| **适应变化** | The team adapted the plan when the integration broke. |
| **趋势预警** | There is an upward trend in integration defects. |
