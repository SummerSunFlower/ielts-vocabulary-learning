练习一：词汇填空（基础巩固）

1.1 趋势描述词（Changes 1）

填入适当的词汇（increase, rise, go up, peak at, reach a peak of, decrease, decline, drop, fall, go down, fluctuate, remain constant, remain steady）：

  1. After the fix, error rate __________ from 8% to 1.2% within an hour.
  2. API latency __________ steadily from 90ms to 140ms during the traffic surge.
  3. Traffic __________ at 14:00 and then started to taper off.
  4. CPU utilization __________ between 40% and 70% throughout the night.
  5. Uptime __________ steady at 99.95% for the whole quarter.
  6. The number of open incidents __________ a peak of 47 during the outage.
  7. Once caching was enabled, database load __________ dramatically.
  8. Daily active users __________ constant despite the marketing pause.

1.2 程度副词（Changes 1）

填入适当的程度副词（dramatically, sharply, steadily, gradually, slightly）：

  1. Response time improved __________ after we upgraded the database instance.
  2. The queue length grew __________ as the batch job stalled.
  3. Memory usage crept up __________ over several hours before the OOM kill.
  4. Throughput dropped __________ when the rate limiter kicked in.
  5. The metric moved only __________ between 09:00 and 10:00.

1.3 状态变化动词（Changes 2）

填入适当的动词（adjust, alter, deteriorate, switch, vary, reduce, replace, transform, improve, expand, adapt, cut）：

  1. We __________ to a different monitoring tool after the old one kept missing alerts.
  2. The legacy billing system was __________ by a cloud-native platform.
  3. Service stability began to __________ after the bad deploy.
  4. To meet the deadline, we __________ two low-priority features from the release.
  5. We __________ the retry timeout to avoid cascading failures.
  6. The on-call roster was __________ to cover the holiday peak.
  7. Error handling __________ significantly once we added structured logging.
  8. The runbook had to be __________ to the new incident process.
  9. Customer satisfaction __________ a lot by region and by channel.
  10. The migration __________ the way we ship software to production.

练习二：句子改写（从口语到精确）

2.1 用趋势词改写

目标：用更精确的动词替换笼统表达

  1. 原句：The number of errors got bigger after the release.
    改写：The number of errors __________ after the release.
  2. 原句：Latency went down a lot when we cached the responses.
    改写：Latency __________ when we cached the responses.
  3. 原句：The load went up and down all night.
    改写：The load __________ all night.
  4. 原句：We hit the highest point of 12k requests at noon.
    改写：Traffic __________ at noon.
  5. 原句：The graph stayed the same for the whole month.
    改写：The metric __________ for the whole month.

2.2 用状态动词改写

目标：用 Changes 2 动词让表达更专业

  1. 原句：We changed the old server to a new one.
    改写：We __________ the old server with a new one.
  2. 原句：The process got better after the retro actions.
    改写：The process __________ after the retro actions.
  3. 原句：We made the team bigger for the launch.
    改写：We __________ the team for the launch.
  4. 原句：The incident got worse before we rolled back.
    改写：The incident __________ before we rolled back.
  5. 原句：We moved the workload to a different region.
    改写：We __________ the workload to a different region.

练习三：情境对话（模拟真实工作）

3.1 故障指标通报（Application Support）

场景：在 Slack 向 Dev 团队通报错误率飙升

Support: Hey @dev-team, error rate on checkout __________ (1) from 1% to 8% in the last 20 minutes. This is __________ (2) about 3,000 orders.

Dev Lead: What changed?

Support: Looks like it started right after the 14:00 deploy. Latency also __________ (3) sharply.

Dev Lead: Probably the new payment call. Let's rollback.

Support: Agreed. Once we roll back, I expect error rate to __________ (4) back to normal.

3.2 进度趋势汇报（PM）

场景：向 Sponsor 汇报进度偏差扩大

PM: The schedule variance has __________ (1) from 5 to 12 days. The root cause is the vendor API delay.

Sponsor: Can we recover?

PM: We __________ (2) two backend engineers from the reporting stream to protect the critical path. Velocity should __________ (3) gradually next sprint.

Sponsor: And the risk rating?

PM: It __________ (4) from high to medium after the dry-run passed.

3.3 系统迁移说明（Postmortem 书面）

场景：RCA 中描述架构替换前后

RCA: During the incident, error rate __________ (1) and stayed above 10% for 40 minutes. The legacy proxy was __________ (2) by the new gateway last month, but the config had not been tuned. After we __________ (3) the timeout settings, stability __________ (4) and the trend returned to baseline.

练习四：错误改正（识别并修正）

4.1 找出错误并修正

找出句子中的用法错误，并给出正确版本：

  1. ❌ The traffic rised to 10k at noon.
    ✅ __________
  2. ❌ Latency reduced down after the fix.
    ✅ __________
  3. ❌ We replaced the old tool to a new one.
    ✅ __________
  4. ❌ The metric remained steadily during the test.
    ✅ __________
  5. ❌ The system was transformed by our new process.
    ✅ __________

4.2 选择最佳表达

从选项中选择最合适的表达（考虑正式程度和语境）：

  1. [正式 RCA 报告]
    A. Errors went up a lot.
    B. The error rate increased sharply.
    C. We saw more errors.
    最佳选择：__________
  2. [Slack 快速更新]
    A. The metric remained steady at 99.9%.
    B. Flatline at 99.9%, all good.
    C. Uptime continued without change at 99.9%.
    最佳选择：__________
  3. [管理层汇报]
    A. We changed the plan.
    B. We adapted the plan to the new constraints.
    C. Plan changed.
    最佳选择：__________

练习五：翻译练习（中译英）

5.1 基础翻译

将中文句子翻译成英文，使用适当的目标词汇：

  1. 部署后错误率从 8% 降到了 1.2%。（使用：drop）
    __________
  2. 流量在下午两点达到峰值。（使用：peak at）
    __________
  3. 我们把旧系统替换成了新的微服务架构。（使用：replace）
    __________
  4. 修复后服务稳定性逐步改善。（使用：improve gradually）
    __________
  5. 由于营销暂停，日活用户保持不变。（使用：remain constant）
    __________

5.2 场景翻译

将场景描述翻译成英文，要求完整、专业：

  1. 场景：Slack 通报故障
    结账服务的错误率在过去 20 分钟从 1% 升到了 8%，影响了大约 3000 笔订单。已经定位到 14:00 的部署。

  2. 场景：RCA 结论
    事故期间错误率急剧上升并在 10% 以上维持了 40 分钟。根因是旧代理被新网关替换后配置未被调优。调整超时设置后稳定性恢复。
    __________
  3. 场景：PM 进度汇报
    进度偏差从 5 天扩大到 12 天，因为供应商接口延迟。我们从报告线调来两名后端工程师保护关键路径，速率应在下个 sprint 逐步回升。
    __________

✅ 参考答案

练习一：词汇填空

1.1 趋势描述词

  1. dropped / fell / decreased / went down
  2. rose / increased / went up
  3. peaked / reach a peak of
  4. fluctuated
  5. remained / remained steady
  6. reached
  7. dropped / decreased / fell
  8. remained / remained constant

1.2 程度副词

  1. dramatically / sharply
  2. steadily
  3. gradually
  4. sharply / dramatically
  5. slightly

1.3 状态变化动词

  1. switched
  2. replaced
  3. deteriorate
  4. cut
  5. adjusted
  6. expanded
  7. improved
  8. adapted
  9. varies
  10. transformed

练习二：句子改写

  1. The number of errors increased / rose / went up after the release.
  2. Latency dropped / decreased / fell sharply when we cached the responses.
  3. The load fluctuated all night.
  4. Traffic peaked at 12k requests at noon. / Traffic reached a peak of 12k at noon.
  5. The metric remained constant / remained steady for the whole month.
  6. We replaced the old server with a new one.
  7. The process improved after the retro actions.
  8. We expanded the team for the launch.
  9. The incident deteriorated before we rolled back.
  10. We switched the workload to a different region.

练习三：情境对话

  1. (1) increased / rose / went up (2) affecting (3) rose / increased (4) drop / fall / decrease
  2. (1) increased / grown / expanded (2) moved / reassigned (3) improve / recover (4) dropped / fell / decreased
  3. (1) peaked / increased (2) replaced (3) adjusted (4) improved / recovered

练习四：错误改正

  1. "rised" 不是词；正确：The traffic rose to 10k at noon. / Traffic peaked at 10k at noon.
  2. reduced 不与 down 连用；正确：Latency dropped after the fix. / Latency reduced after the fix.
  3. replace 搭配 with/by，不与 to；正确：We replaced the old tool with a new one.
  4. remained 后接形容词 steady/constant，不用副词 steadily；正确：The metric remained steady during the test.
  5. transform 作"改变"多为及物，且语义过重；此处宜用 improved/changed。正确：The system was improved by our new process.
  1. B（正式报告用 increased sharply 最精确）
  2. B（Slack 快速更新用简短语体）
  3. B（管理层汇报用 adapted 显专业）

练习五：翻译练习

  1. After the deploy, the error rate dropped from 8% to 1.2%.
  2. Traffic peaked at 2 PM. / Traffic peaked at 14:00.
  3. We replaced the legacy system with the new microservices architecture.
  4. Service stability improved gradually after the fix.
  5. Daily active users remained constant due to the marketing pause.
  6. Error rate on the checkout service increased from 1% to 8% in the last 20 minutes, affecting about 3,000 orders. We traced it to the 2 PM deploy.
  7. During the incident, the error rate increased sharply and remained above 10% for 40 minutes. Root cause was that the legacy proxy was replaced by the new gateway without the config being tuned. After we adjusted the timeout settings, stability recovered.
  8. The schedule variance expanded from 5 to 12 days because of the vendor API delay. We moved two backend engineers from the reporting stream to protect the critical path, and velocity should improve gradually next sprint.

📝 学习建议

循序渐进

  • 第一周：完成练习一（词汇填空）+ 练习四（错误改正）
  • 第二周：完成练习二（句子改写）+ 练习五（翻译练习）
  • 第三周：完成练习三（情境对话）
  • 第四周：综合应用 + 复习全部，准备严格测试

每日练习

  • ⏰ 15-20 分钟/天：1-2 个练习
  • 📊 工作中刻意用：写 status report / RCA 时刻意用趋势词替代 "went up / went down"
  • 🎯 实战应用：用 increase/rise/drop/fall/peak at 描述真实指标

进阶技巧

  • ✍ 口头练习：朗读例句，强化语感
  • 💬 场景模拟：自问自答，模拟故障/进度通报
  • 📝 写作练习：用学到的词汇写一段 incident RCA 或周报
  • 🔊 录音对比：录下自己的表达，对比标准答案
