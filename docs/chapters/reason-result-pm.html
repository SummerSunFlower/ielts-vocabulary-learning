📌 核心词汇框架

词类分类

词类 含义 PM 场景应用
原因类 解释"为什么" 延期原因、里程碑偏差解释、决策依据
目的类 表达"为了" 资源调配目的、流程改进初衷、缓解措施意图
结果类 描述"结果" 延期影响、依赖阻塞后果、交付效果
动机类 深层原因 范围取舍动机、优先级排序逻辑
影响类 描述"对…产生影响" 风险对关键路径的影响、变更对范围的影响

🔵 原因类词汇 — 延期解释与决策依据

词汇 用法 示例
due to + 名词短语，"由于" The release is delayed due to unresolved integration issues.
owing to + 名词短语，"归因于" The milestone slipped owing to a dependency on the vendor team.
on account of + 名词短语，"因为" Testing was postponed on account of the environment outage.
the reason for + 名词/动名词，"…的原因" What was the reason for the two-day delay in UAT sign-off?
on the grounds that + 从句，"基于…的理由" We descoped the module on the grounds that it was not on the critical path.

💡 PM 场景提示： 这五个词在 status report、变更申请（Change Request）和风险登记册（Risk Register）中高频出现。

The release is delayed due to unresolved integration issues between the payment service and the loyalty API.
（本次发布因支付服务与会员积分 API 之间未解决的集成问题而延期。）

We deferred the data migration on the grounds that it carried unacceptable regression risk before go-live.
（我们推迟了数据迁移，理由是它在上线前带有不可接受的回归风险。）

🟡 目的类词汇 — 决策与行动目的说明

词汇 用法 示例
in order to + 动词原形，"为了"（正式） We reallocated two engineers in order to protect the critical path.
so as not to + 动词原形，"为了不" We froze the code base so as not to destabilize the release candidate.
with the aim of + 动名词，"目的是" We introduced a weekly risk review with the aim of catching blockers earlier.

💡 PM 场景提示： 说明资源调配、流程变更、缓解措施（mitigation）的合理性时使用，是向 sponsor 争取支持的利器。

We scheduled the cutover rehearsal on Saturday so as not to disrupt the business-critical batch jobs.
（我们把切换演练安排在周六，以免干扰业务关键的批处理任务。）

🟢 结果类词汇 — 影响评估与交付效果

词汇 用法 示例
ensued v. （不及物动词）随之发生 A two-week delay ensued after the vendor missed the interface deadline.
as a result 连接词短语，"结果是" The API contract changed late, and as a result our test cases had to be rewritten.
as a consequence 连接词短语，"因此" The integration slipped, and as a consequence UAT started one week late.
affect v. 影响（及物，不加 to） This delay will affect the go-live date and the marketing campaign launch.

💡 PM 场景提示： 在 impact assessment（影响评估）和干系人汇报中量化"拖延的代价"。

If the vendor misses the next interface deadline, further slippage will ensue across all downstream milestones.
（如果供应商错过下一个接口交付期限，所有下游里程碑都会随之进一步延期。）

🔴 动机/后果类词汇 — 干系人沟通与取舍说明

词汇 含义 示例
motive n. 动机，意图 What was the motive behind pulling the feature into this release?
effect n. 效果，影响 The daily sync had an immediate positive effect on dependency resolution.
consequence n. 后果 We must weigh the consequences of going live with known defects.

🎯 场景一：延期通知（Delay Notification）

流程： 进度现状 → 延期原因 → 影响评估 → 补救措施 → 新预期

步骤 模板 示例
进度现状 Current status: X is behind schedule by... Current status: the integration workstream is behind schedule by two weeks.
延期原因 This is due to / owing to... This is due to unresolved integration issues on the vendor side.
影响评估 As a consequence, X will affect Y... As a consequence, this will affect UAT and push go-live to Oct 15.
补救措施 We are doing X in order to... We are adding a contractor in order to compress the remaining test cycle.
新预期 As a result, we expect... As a result, we expect to recover three days before the next milestone.

完整示例： Current status: the release is delayed by two weeks due to unresolved integration issues between the order service and the vendor's inventory API. As a consequence, UAT will start one week later and affect the October go-live window. We have reallocated two engineers and engaged the vendor's escalation manager in order to protect the remaining milestones. As a result, we expect to recover three days by the next status meeting.

🎯 场景二：风险上报（Risk Escalation）

流程： 风险描述 → 触发原因 → 影响评估 → 缓解措施 → 预期效果

步骤 模板
风险描述 There is a risk that X may occur...
触发原因 This risk arises owing to / on account of...
影响评估 If X occurs, as a consequence, ...
缓解措施 We are doing Y with the aim of...
预期效果 As a result, we expect to reduce the impact to...

完整示例： There is a risk that the legacy data migration will overrun its window. This risk arises owing to undocumented data quality issues in the old system. If the overrun occurs, as a consequence the cutover will extend into Monday morning and affect day-one operations. We are running a full dry-run migration this weekend with the aim of validating the timing. As a result, we expect to reduce the residual risk to low before go-live.

💬 对话一：向 Sponsor 解释延期两周（含补救方案）

PM: Thanks for making time, Sarah. I need to walk you through a schedule change on the lululemon integration release.

Sponsor: Okay, give it to me straight. How bad is it?

PM: The release is delayed by two weeks due to unresolved integration issues between our order service and the vendor's inventory API.

Sponsor: Two weeks? I presented October 1st to the board last month. What exactly went wrong?

PM: The vendor delivered the interface spec late, and when we integrated it, the payload format didn't match what we'd built against. Rework ensued on both sides.

Sponsor: Why didn't we catch that earlier?

PM: Fair question. We had contract tests, but they ran against a mock. The mock was built from the early draft spec owing to a version mix-up on the vendor's portal.

Sponsor: So what's the impact beyond the two weeks?

PM: As a consequence, UAT shifts by one week and go-live moves to October 15th. It doesn't affect the holiday freeze in November, so we still have buffer.

Sponsor: And what are you doing about it?

PM: Two things. First, we reallocated two backend engineers in order to protect the critical path. Second, we've asked the vendor to assign a dedicated engineer to our account.

Sponsor: What's the motive behind pulling the engineers off the reporting workstream?

PM: Simple trade-off: reporting isn't on the critical path, integration is. We made that call on the grounds that protecting go-live matters more than a parallel feature.

Sponsor: I see. Are you confident in the new date?

PM: Reasonably. We've added a mid-point checkpoint in ten days with the aim of catching any further drift early. As a result of the reallocation, we expect to recover three of the fourteen days.

Sponsor: Okay. Send me the revised plan today, and flag it in the weekly report as amber, not red.

PM: Will do. I'll update the risk register too, so as not to let the vendor dependency slip through untracked again.

💬 对话二：风险评审会（上报集成风险，节选）

PM: Next up is the integration risk I logged last week. There is a real risk that the migration overruns its weekend window. The trigger is owing to undocumented data quality issues in the legacy system.

Sponsor: How likely is that, honestly?

PM: Moderate probability, high impact. If the overrun occurs, as a consequence the cutover extends into Monday morning, which will affect day-one store operations.

PM: So we run a full dry-run migration this weekend with the aim of validating the actual timing against our estimate.

Tech Lead: I'd also add a dedup pass before the real cutover in order to shrink the dataset.

PM: Then if the dry-run still shows we can't finish in the window, we go with a phased cutover. We'd choose that on the grounds that a shorter window per batch is far lower risk than one long one. As a result of these mitigations, I'll downgrade the residual risk from high to medium after the dry-run results come in.

📊 PM 场景速查表

进度汇报（周报 / Status Update）

想表达 常用表达
进度延期 The release is delayed / behind schedule due to...
偏差原因 The variance is owing to / on account of...
影响评估 As a consequence, this will affect X / push go-live to...
补救措施 We are doing X in order to protect the critical path.
新预期 As a result, we expect to recover X days by...

风险上报（Risk Register / 评审会）

想表达 常用表达
描述风险 There is a risk that X may occur...
触发原因 This risk arises owing to / on account of...
潜在后果 If X occurs, as a consequence, ...
缓解措施 We are doing Y with the aim of reducing the impact.
决策理由 We chose the phased approach on the grounds that...

资源协调（借调 / Descope / 变更）

想表达 常用表达
说明目的 We reallocated two engineers in order to unblock integration.
说明动机 The motive behind the descope was to keep go-live fixed.
说明理由 We deferred X on the grounds that it's not on the critical path.
