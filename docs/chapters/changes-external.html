📌 核心词汇框架

一、SLA / 指标趋势通报（Changes 1 — 描述数据变化）

词类 含义 对外沟通应用
上升动词 指标增长 通知客户吞吐、可用性上升
下降动词 指标回落 通报延迟、事故数下降
波动/持平 不稳定/稳定 说明指标在合理范围内波动或保持平稳
程度副词 变化幅度 量化变化剧烈程度（急剧/逐步/轻微）
趋势名词 总体走向 SLA 月度回顾中的整体趋势描述

二、交付范围 / 合作状态变化（Changes 2 — 状态/性质变化动词）

词类 含义 对外沟通应用
调整类 调整/改变/适应 交付范围调整、流程适配
增减类 扩大/削减/减少 支持范围扩缩、成本优化
转换类 切换/替换/转型 供应商切换、方案替换、架构转型
状态类 改善/恶化/消失 服务质量改善、风险消退

📈 SLA / 指标趋势通报词汇（Changes 1）

词汇 用法 示例
increase v./n. 上升 API throughput increased from 1,200 to 1,800 req/s after scaling out.
（横向扩容后，API 吞吐从 1,200 提升到 1,800 请求/秒。）
rise v. 上升 The availability rose to 99.95% in the last reporting period.
（在上一个报告周期，可用性上升到 99.95%。）
go up v. 上升（口语偏多） The monthly incident count went up by 12% during the migration.
（迁移期间，月度事故数上升了 12%。）
peak at v. 在…达到峰值 Traffic peaked at 9,500 concurrent users at 20:00 UTC.
（流量在 UTC 时间 20:00 达到 9,500 并发用户的峰值。）
reach a peak of v. 达到…的峰值 The system reached a peak of 2.1 million requests per minute.
（系统达到了每分钟 210 万请求的峰值。）
decrease v./n. 下降 The error rate decreased from 1.8% to 0.4% after the fix.
（修复后错误率从 1.8% 下降到 0.4%。）
decline v./n. 下降 The number of open tickets declined quarter over quarter.
（未结工单数逐季度下降。）
drop v. 下降（常指骤降） API latency dropped from 320ms to 210ms after the optimization.
（优化后 API 延迟从 320ms 降到 210ms。）
fall v. 下降 Average response time fell below the 250ms SLA threshold.
（平均响应时间降到了 250ms 的 SLA 阈值以下。）
go down v. 下降（口语偏多） The weekly downtime went down from 40 to 12 minutes.
（每周停机时间从 40 分钟降到 12 分钟。）
fluctuate v. 波动 The queue depth fluctuates between 50 and 200 during business hours.
（队列深度在营业时段于 50 到 200 之间波动。）
remain constant v. 保持不变 The CPU utilization remained constant at around 45% under load.
（在负载下，CPU 使用率保持在约 45% 不变。）
remain steady v. 保持平稳 Availability remained steady at 99.9% throughout the quarter.
（整个季度可用性保持在 99.9% 平稳水平。）
dramatically adv. 急剧地 Incident volume dropped dramatically after the patch.
（补丁发布后事故量急剧下降。）
sharply adv. sharply/急剧地 Latency fell sharply once the cache was enabled.
（启用缓存后延迟急剧下降。）
steadily adv. 稳定地 Throughput increased steadily week by week.
（吞吐逐周稳定增长。）
gradually adv. 逐步地 The error rate declined gradually as the team tuned the service.
（随着团队调优服务，错误率逐步下降。）
slightly adv. 轻微地 Response time increased slightly during peak hours.
（高峰时段响应时间轻微上升。）
upward trend n. 上升趋势 We observe an upward trend in monthly active integrations.
（我们观察到月度活跃集成数呈上升趋势。）
downward trend n. 下降趋势 There is a clear downward trend in support ticket volume.
（支持工单量有明显的下降趋势。）

💡 对外沟通提示： 通报 SLA 指标时，先给方向动词（rise / drop），再用程度副词（sharply / gradually）说明幅度，最后用趋势名词（upward / downward trend）收尾整体走向。

API latency dropped sharply from 320ms to 210ms after the optimization, and availability rose to 99.95%.
（优化后 API 延迟急剧从 320ms 降到 210ms，可用性上升到 99.95%。）

Across the quarter, throughput increased steadily, showing a clear upward trend in service capacity.
（整个季度吞吐稳定增长，显示出服务能力的明显上升趋势。）

🔧 交付范围 / 合作状态变化动词（Changes 2）

词汇 用法 示例
adjust v. 调整/适应 We adjusted the delivery timeline to match the client's new launch date.
（我们调整了交付时间表以配合客户新的发布日期。）
alter v. 改变 The vendor altered the API contract, which required re-testing.
（供应商改变了 API 契约，这需要重新测试。）
deteriorate v. 恶化 Service quality deteriorated before the root cause was found.
（在找到根因前，服务质量出现了恶化。）
exchange v. 交换 We exchanged the on-site engineer for a remote support pod.
（我们将现场工程师换成了远程支持小组。）
fade v. 逐渐消失 The initial performance concerns faded after the tuning work.
（调优工作完成后，最初的性担忧逐渐消失。）
reduce v. 减少 We reduced the support window to business hours only.
（我们将支持时段缩减为仅限营业时间。）
renovate v. 翻新 The vendor will renovate the legacy reporting portal next quarter.
（供应商将在下季度翻新遗留报表门户。）
swell v. 膨胀/增大 The backlog swelled during the holiday peak and strained the team.
（假期高峰期间积压量增大，给团队带来压力。）
switch / switch to v. 转换 We switched to a managed message queue to cut operational load.
（我们切换到托管消息队列以降低运维负担。）
vary v. 变化/不同 Response times vary by region depending on edge node coverage.
（响应时间因边缘节点覆盖情况而随地区不同。）
adapt v. 适应/改编 The team adapted the runbook to the client's incident process.
（团队将操作手册适配为客户方的事故流程。）
cut v. 削减 The client cut the non-critical scope to protect the go-live date.
（客户削减了非关键范围以保障上线日期。）
decline v. 下降/拒绝 The vendor declined the change request due to capacity limits.
（供应商因产能限制拒绝了该变更请求。）
disappear v. 消失 The weekend outage risk disappeared after the failover upgrade.
（故障转移升级后，周末宕机风险消失了。）
expand v. 扩张 We expanded the support scope to cover weekend on-call.
（我们扩大了支持范围以覆盖周末值守。）
improve v. 改善 The new pipeline improved deployment speed by 40%.
（新流水线将部署速度改善了 40%。）
promote v. 提升/晋升 We promoted the candidate build to production after UAT passed.
（UAT 通过后，我们将候选构建提升到了生产环境。）
relax v. 放松 The client agreed to relax the latency SLA from 200ms to 300ms.
（客户同意将延迟 SLA 从 200ms 放宽到 300ms。）
replace v. 替换 We replaced the old gateway with a cloud-native proxy.
（我们用云原生代理替换了旧网关。）
transform v. 彻底转型 The engagement transformed from break-fix to proactive monitoring.
（合作从救火式支持彻底转型为主动监控。）

💡 对外沟通提示： 范围与状态变化属于"变更沟通"，务必用过去式/完成时陈述已发生事实，用将来时说明计划，避免模糊措辞。

We expanded the delivery scope and replaced the legacy connector with a newer integration.
（我们扩大了交付范围，并用更新的集成替换了遗留连接器。）

The partnership transformed from reactive support into a joint reliability program.
（合作从被动支持彻底转型为联合可靠性项目。）

🎯 场景一：SLA 指标变化通知（SLA Metric Change Notice）

流程： 指标现状 → 变化方向 → 业务影响 → 后续动作

指标 变化 影响 后续
API latency dropped from 320ms to 210ms 用户体验提升，达标 SLA 持续监控并纳入月度报告
Availability rose to 99.95% 超过 99.9% 承诺值 保持当前架构不变
Incident count declined by 30% 运维负担减轻 复盘根因并固化措施
Throughput increased steadily to 1,800 req/s 可承载更大流量 规划下一步扩容

完整示例（邮件风）： Dear client, we are pleased to share the latest SLA results. API latency dropped from 320ms to 210ms after the optimization, and availability rose to 99.95%, exceeding our committed 99.9%. The monthly incident count declined by 30%, while throughput increased steadily to 1,800 requests per second. These improvements reduced user-facing errors and strengthened the overall service stability. Going forward, we will keep monitoring the metrics and report the upward trend in our monthly review. Please let us know if you would like a deeper breakdown by region.
（尊敬的客户，我们很高兴分享最新的 SLA 结果。优化后 API 延迟从 320ms 降到 210ms，可用性上升到 99.95%，超过了我们承诺的 99.9%。月度事故数下降了 30%，而吞吐稳定增长到每秒 1,800 请求。这些改善减少了面向用户的错误并增强了整体服务稳定性。后续我们将持续监控指标，并在月度回顾中汇报这一上升趋势。如需按地区细分，请告知我们。）

🎯 场景二：交付范围调整与期望管理（Scope & Expectation）

流程： 现状说明 → 范围变化 → 替代方案 → 期望对齐 → 后续

步骤 模板 示例
现状说明 We reviewed the current delivery scope and found... We reviewed the current delivery scope and found the original plan overcommitted.
范围变化 We reduced / expanded / replaced / transformed... We reduced the non-critical modules and expanded the monitoring coverage.
替代方案 We replaced X with Y and adapted Z to... We replaced the manual report with an automated dashboard and adapted the runbook.
期望对齐 This transforms our collaboration from X to Y. This transformed our collaboration from break-fix into proactive support.
后续 We will confirm the revised expectation by... We will confirm the revised expectation in the next steering meeting.

完整示例： Dear client, following our review, we reduced the non-critical reporting modules to protect the go-live date, and expanded the support scope to cover weekend on-call. We replaced the manual export with an automated dashboard, and adapted the runbook to your incident process. This transformed the engagement from reactive support into a proactive reliability program. As a result, we expect faster mean-time-to-recover and clearer SLAs. Please confirm if this revised scope matches your expectation before we finalize the contract addendum.
（尊敬的客户，经评审后，我们削减了非关键报表模块以保障上线日期，并扩大了支持范围以覆盖周末值守。我们用自动化看板替换了手工导出，并将操作手册适配为贵方的事故流程。这把合作从被动支持彻底转型为主动可靠性项目。因此，我们预期故障恢复时间更短、SLA 更清晰。在最终确定合同附录前，请确认此修订范围是否符合贵方期望。）

💬 对话一：与客户同步可用性指标改善

Us: Thanks for the call. I'd like to walk you through the latest SLA numbers for the quarter.

Client: Great. How is availability looking after the failover work?

Us: Availability remained steady at 99.9% through the first two months, then improved to 99.95% in month three after we tuned the database pool.

Client: That's solid. What about latency, since our users complained last quarter?

Us: API latency dropped from 320ms to 210ms, and the error rate reduced from 1.8% to 0.4% once the cache was enabled.

Client: Excellent. Any risk this regresses?

Us: We've added synthetic monitoring, so if metrics drift we'll catch it early. The upward trend should hold into next quarter.

Client: Perfect. Please include this in the monthly report so my stakeholders see the progress.

💬 对话二：与供应商沟通切换方案

Us: We'd like to discuss the integration approach for the next phase with you.

Vendor: Of course. What are you proposing?

Us: We switched to a managed message queue on our side, and we plan to replace the legacy connector with your newer SDK.

Vendor: The newer SDK is a good call. Our old connector is end-of-life.

Us: Exactly. Would you be able to support the migration in parallel, or do you need to phase it?

Vendor: We'll have to decline the parallel track due to capacity, but we can phase it over two sprints.

Us: Understood. If we phase it, do you think the overall reliability will still improve by the end of the quarter?

Vendor: Yes, once the SDK is fully rolled out, you should see latency and error rates improve noticeably.
