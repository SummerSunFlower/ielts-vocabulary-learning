📌 核心词汇框架

词类分类

词类 含义 敏捷场景应用
速率 / 燃尽趋势类 描述数值的上升、下降、波动、持平 sprint velocity、burndown、WIP、defect count 的汇报与趋势判断
流程 / 状态改进类 描述过程、性质、状态的变化 流程改进、Definition of Done、团队规模、质量门、回顾项落地
趋势名词类 概括"上升 / 下降"的方向 在 Sprint Review 总结周期走向：upward / downward trend
程度副词类 修饰变化的幅度与速度 dramatically、sharply、steadily、gradually、slightly 量化变化强度

📈 速率与燃尽趋势词汇（Changes 1）

词汇 用法 示例
increase v. / n. 上升 Velocity increased from 32 to 41 points over three sprints.
（速率三个 sprint 从 32 稳步升到 41 点。）
rise v. 上升 The number of story points completed rose week over week.
（完成的用户故事点数逐周上升。）
go up 短语动词，上升 Our burndown showed remaining work going up after the scope change.
（范围变更后燃尽图显示剩余工作量上升。）
peak at 短语动词，达到峰值 Open defects peaked at 23 on day nine of the sprint.
（未关闭缺陷在 sprint 第 9 天达到峰值 23 个。）
reach a peak of 短语，达到…的峰值 The WIP reached a peak of 14 items before we limited it.
（在设限之前，在制品数量达到峰值 14 项。）
decrease v. / n. 下降 Cycle time decreased after we trimmed handoffs.
（削减交接环节后，周期时间下降。）
decline v. 下降 The defect rate declined steadily across the release.
（整个发布周期缺陷率稳步下降。）
drop v. 下降 Deployment frequency dropped during the code freeze.
（冻结期内部署频率下降。）
fall v. 下降 Carryover tasks fell from nine to three this sprint.
（遗留任务本 sprint 从 9 个降到 3 个。）
go down 短语动词，下降 The escaped-defect count went down after the new quality gate.
（新质量门引入后逃逸缺陷数下降。）
fluctuate v. 波动 WIP fluctuated between six and eleven items all sprint.
（整个 sprint 在制品在 6 到 11 项之间波动。）
remain constant 短语，保持不变 The team size remained constant at seven engineers.
（团队规模保持在 7 名工程师不变。）
remain steady 短语，保持稳定 Velocity remained steady around 38 points per sprint.
（速率稳定在每 sprint 约 38 点。）
dramatically adv. 大幅地 Escaped bugs dropped dramatically after the review gate.
（新评审门后逃逸缺陷大幅下降。）
sharply adv. 急剧地 Open defects fell sharply in the week after release.
（发布后那一周未关闭缺陷急剧下降。）
steadily adv. 稳步地 Velocity rose steadily over four consecutive sprints.
（速率连续四个 sprint 稳步上升。）
gradually adv. 逐渐地 Lead time improved gradually as the pipeline matured.
（随流水线成熟，前置时间逐步改善。）
slightly adv. 略微地 The burndown slope changed slightly after replanning.
（重新规划后燃尽斜率略有变化。）
upward trend n. 上升趋势 There is a clear upward trend in our deployment frequency.
（我们的部署频率呈明显的上升趋势。）
downward trend n. 下降趋势 We see a downward trend in cycle time this quarter.
（本季度周期时间呈下降趋势。）

💡 敏捷场景提示： 这 20 个词是 Sprint Review 里用数据说话的核心工具；搭配 dramatically / steadily 等副词，能精确传达变化幅度。

Velocity increased steadily from 32 to 41 points over three sprints.
（速率三个 sprint 从 32 稳步升到 41 点。）

Open defects peaked at 23 on day nine, then fell sharply after the hardening week.
（未关闭缺陷第 9 天达到峰值 23，之后在加固周急剧下降。）

🔧 流程与状态改进动词（Changes 2）

Group 1 — 状态 / 性质变化

词汇 用法 示例
adjust v. 调整 / 适应 We adjusted the sprint length to two weeks after the pilot.
（试点后我们把 sprint 长度调整为两周。）
alter v. 改变 The Definition of Done was altered following the process audit.
（流程审计后"完成定义"被修改。）
deteriorate v. 恶化 Code quality deteriorated when WIP was consistently too high.
（在制品长期过高时代码质量恶化。）
exchange v. 交换 The two teams exchanged a reviewer each sprint for cross-checking.
（两个团队每个 sprint 互换一名评审做交叉检查。）
fade v. 逐渐消失 / 褪色 Interest in the old release train faded after the weekly pilot.
（周级试点后，大家对旧发布节奏的兴趣逐渐消退。）
reduce v. 减少 We reduced WIP to five to keep the flow predictable.
（我们把在制品减少到 5，以保持流动可预测。）
renovate v. 翻新 We renovated the onboarding wiki for new developers.
（我们翻新了面向新人的入职 wiki。）
swell v. 膨胀 / 增大 The backlog swelled after the discovery workshop.
（探索工作坊后待办清单膨胀。）
switch v. 转换 We switched the planning tool mid-quarter.
（我们在季度中切换了规划工具。）
switch to 短语，转向 We switched to trunk-based development last month.
（上月我们转向了主干开发。）
vary v. 变化 / 不同 Sprint goals vary by team maturity and domain risk.
（sprint 目标随团队成熟度与领域风险而不同。）

Group 2 — 改进 / 转型

词汇 用法 示例
adapt v. 适应 / 改编 The standup format adapted well to remote work.
（站会形式很好地适应了远程工作。）
cut v. 削减 We cut the release train from monthly to weekly.
（我们把发布节奏从月级削减到周级。）
decline v. 下降 / 拒绝 The proposal was declined by the steering group.
（该提案被指导委员会拒绝。）
disappear v. 消失 Hand-off delays disappeared after we co-located the teams.
（团队集中办公后交接延迟消失了。）
expand v. 扩张 The squad expanded from five to eight members.
（小组从 5 人扩张到 8 人。）
improve v. 改善 We improved the build time with a shared cache.
（我们用共享缓存改善了构建时间。）
promote v. 提升 / 晋升 She was promoted to tech lead after the successful launch.
（成功发布后她被提升为技术负责人。）
relax v. 放松 We relaxed the WIP limit during the crunch period.
（在紧张期我们放宽了在制品限制。）
replace v. 替换 We replaced the daily status meeting with async updates.
（我们用异步更新取代了每日状态会议。）
transform v. 彻底转型 / 改变 The retro helped us transform how we plan capacity.
（回顾会帮助我们彻底改变了规划产能的方式。）

💡 敏捷场景提示： Group 1 描述"状态变了"，Group 2 描述"变好了 / 转型了"；在 Retrospective 里高频出现。

We replaced the daily status meeting with an async update to save time.
（我们用异步更新取代每日状态会议以节省时间。）

The retro action transformed our planning cadence within two sprints.
（这条回顾项在两个 sprint 内彻底改变了我们的规划节奏。）

🎯 场景一：Sprint Review 速率汇报

流程： 指标 → 趋势 → 结论

指标 趋势 结论
Velocity rose steadily from 32 to 41 points 团队交付能力增强，可承诺更大 sprint goal
Burndown remained steady after mid-sprint 剩余工作量按预期燃烧，无重大偏差
Open defects peaked at 23, then fell sharply 加固周见效，质量回归可控
WIP fluctuated between 6 and 11 流动不够平稳，需在 Retro 设 WIP 上限

完整示例： In this sprint review, velocity increased steadily from 32 to 41 story points, showing a clear upward trend in our delivery capacity. The burndown remained steady after day six, so we finished the sprint goal on time. Open defects peaked at 23 on day nine but dropped dramatically after the hardening week, which is a downward trend we want to keep. However, WIP fluctuated between six and eleven items, so we will add a WIP limit next sprint.

🎯 场景二：Retrospective 改进项

流程： 问题 → 改进动作 → 预期效果

问题 改进动作 预期效果
每日状态会议耗时 replaced with async updates 每天节省 30 分钟同步时间
交接延迟高 流程 adapted 到集中办公 阻塞时间消失
在制品过多 WIP limit reduced to five 流动更可预测
规划方式陈旧 回顾会 transformed 规划节奏 产能规划更准确

完整示例： From this retro, three actions stand out. First, we replaced the daily status meeting with an async update, which improved our focus time. Second, the planning process adapted to a two-week rhythm, and hand-off delays disappeared. Third, we reduced the WIP limit to five, and that single change transformed how smoothly work flows to done. Overall, throughput improved by 20% and the team feels the cadence is healthier.

💬 对话一：Daily Standup 描述昨日进展与阻塞趋势

Dev A: Yesterday our open-defect count dropped from 12 to 7 after the pair-review session.
（昨天结对评审后，未关闭缺陷从 12 个降到 7 个。）

Dev B: The build time remained steady at about nine minutes, no regression.
（构建时间稳定在约 9 分钟，没有回退。）

Dev A: The review queue fluctuated between three and six PRs through the day.
（评审队列全天在 3 到 6 个 PR 之间波动。）

Scrum Master: Good. To keep flow smooth, we reduced WIP to five and the blocked ticket cleared this morning.
（很好。为保持流动顺畅，我们把在制品降到 5，今早那张阻塞任务也清掉了。）

💬 对话二：Retro 讨论流程改进

Tech Lead: The escape rate declined after we added the pair-review gate.
（加入结对评审门后，逃逸率下降了。）

PM: And the hand-off delay disappeared once we merged the two squads.
（合并两个小组后，交接延迟消失了。）

Dev: Throughput improved by 20% over the last two sprints.
（过去两个 sprint 吞吐量提升了 20%。）

Scrum Master: So this retro should transform our planning cadence — let's move to a fixed two-week rhythm.
（所以本次回顾会应当彻底改变我们的规划节奏——我们改用固定的双周节奏吧。）

📊 敏捷会议速查表

Daily Standup（昨日 / 今日 / 阻塞）

想表达 常用表达
进展上升 Our velocity / completed points rose; open defects dropped from X to Y.
状态持平 The build time remained steady; WIP remained constant at N.
波动 The review queue fluctuated between three and six PRs.
减少阻塞 We reduced WIP to keep the flow smooth.

Sprint Review（趋势汇报）

想表达 常用表达
上升趋势 Velocity increased steadily; there is an upward trend in deployment frequency.
下降趋势 Defects declined; we see a downward trend in cycle time.
峰值 Open defects peaked at 23, then fell sharply.
幅度修饰 Escaped bugs dropped dramatically / slightly after the gate.

Retrospective（改进项）

想表达 常用表达
替换流程 We replaced the status meeting with async updates.
改善 / 适应 The process adapted to remote work; throughput improved by 20%.
削减 / 扩张 We cut the release train to weekly; the squad expanded to eight.
转型 This retro action transformed how we plan capacity.

Open defects __________ at 23 on day nine, but then __________ sharply after the hardening week. However, WIP __________ between six and eleven items, so we will add a WIP limit next sprint.

练习二：Retrospective 填空
