## 📌 考试提示

> 本章词汇特别适用于：
> - **雅思听力（Listening）**：听到有人描述某物的工作原理
> - **雅思写作 Task 1（Writing Part 1）**：看到图表/示意图，需要描述工作原理

---

## 📝 语法要点：主动语态 vs 被动语态

| 情况 | 语态 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| 描述中**无人参与**，物体自然运作 | **主动语态** | …light **enters** the camera… |
| 描述中**有人参与**操作 | **被动语态**（is/are + 过去分词） | The thermostat **is adjusted**… |
| 不知道/不需要说明是谁做的动作 | **被动语态** | …liquid and gas **are compressed**… |

### 1. A Thermostat（恒温器）

**核心词汇：**

| 词汇 | 词性 | 含义 |
|------|------|------|
| contain | v. | 含有，包含 |
| heat up | phrasal v. | 加热，升温 |
| expand | v. | 膨胀，扩张 |
| bend | v. | 弯曲 |
| connect (with) | v. | 与…连接 |
| turn off | phrasal v. | 关闭（电源） |
| cool down | phrasal v. | 冷却，降温 |
| contract | v. | 收缩 |
| disconnect | v. | 断开，切断连接 |
| adjust | v. | 调节，调整 |

**完整描述（含答案）：**

> A thermostat **contains** a strip or coil of steel and a strip or coil of copper, one on top of the other. As the strip/coil **heats up**, the metals **expand**, but one does it faster than the other. The strip/coil **bends** and **connects** with a switch, which **turns off** the power supply. When the strip/coil **cools down**, the metals **contract** and the switch is **disconnected**. The thermostat is **adjusted** using a dial or other control.

### 2. A Disc Player（光盘播放器）

**核心词汇：**

| 词汇 | 词性 | 含义 |
|------|------|------|
| insert | v. | 插入 |
| spin | v. | 旋转 |
| strike | v. | 照射，打到 |
| convert (into) | v. | 转换（为） |
| hear / view | v. | 听到 / 看到 |
| increase / decrease | v. | 增大 / 减小 |
| replace | v. | 取代，替代 |

**完整描述（含答案）：**

> A disc is **inserted** into the player and begins to **spin**. At the same time, a thin beam of light called a laser **strikes** the disc and **converts** digital signals into sounds or images, which can be **heard** through speakers or **viewed** on a screen. Volume or brightness can be **increased** or **decreased** by means of a button, knob or other control. Nowadays, discs are largely being **replaced** by storage devices like memory sticks, which have no moving parts.

### 3. An Aerosol（气雾剂喷罐）

**核心词汇：**

| 词汇 | 词性 | 含义 |
|------|------|------|
| compress | v. | 压缩 |
| release | v. | 释放 |
| push | v. | 按下，推 |
| open | v. | 打开（阀门） |
| leave | v. | 离开，从…出来 |
| mix (with) | v. | 与…混合 |
| expand | v. | 膨胀，扩散 |

**完整描述（含答案）：**

> In an aerosol, liquid and gas are **compressed** in a metal and/or hard plastic tube. This can be **released** from the tube by **pushing** a button, which **opens** a valve. When the liquid-gas combination **leaves** the tube and **mixes** with oxygen, it rapidly **expands**.

### 4. An Aircraft（飞机）

**核心词汇：**

| 词汇 | 词性 | 含义 |
|------|------|------|
| be made of | phrase | 由…制成 |
| move / accelerate | v. | 移动 / 加速 |
| flow | v. | 流动（空气流过） |
| create / produce | v. | 产生，形成 |
| form | v. | 形成 |
| pull | v. | 拉，拽（拉升） |

**重要概念：**
- **thrust**（推力）：发动机产生的向前推力
- **lift**（升力）：使飞机升空的力
- **vacuum**（真空）：机翼上方形成的低压区

**完整描述（含答案）：**

> Most aircraft are **made** of aluminium, and require two forces to allow them to fly: thrust and lift. As the aircraft **moves** forward on the ground under the power of its engines, air **flows** over the wings. As it **accelerates** faster, **creating/producing** more thrust, a vacuum is **created/formed** over the wings. This **creates/produces** lift. The aircraft is **pulled** into the air by the force of this lift.

### 5. A Digital Camera（数码相机）

**核心词汇：**

| 词汇 | 词性 | 含义 |
|------|------|------|
| consist of | phrasal v. | 由…组成 |
| press | v. | 按下（按钮） |
| open | v. | 打开（快门） |
| enter | v. | 进入（光线进入） |
| control | v. | 控制 |
| adjust | v. | 调整 |
| hit | v. | 打到，照射到（传感器） |
| record | v. | 记录 |
| store | v. | 存储 |
| download | v. | 下载，传输到 |

**重要术语：**
- **shutter**（快门）：镜头中控制进光的窗口
- **aperture**（光圈）：控制进光量的小窗口
- **sensor**（传感器）：将光线转换为数字图像的元件
- **memory card**（存储卡）：储存图像的介质

**完整描述（含答案）：**

> A digital camera **consists** of two main parts: a body and a lens. When a button is **pressed** on the body, a window in the lens called a shutter **opens** and light **enters** the camera. The amount of light going into the camera is **controlled** by both the speed of this shutter, and a smaller window called an aperture. Both the shutter speed and the size of the aperture can be **adjusted** by the person using the camera. The light **hits** a sensor in the body of the camera, which **records** the light as a digital image. The image is **recorded/stored** on a memory card in the camera, and this can later be **downloaded** onto a computer.
